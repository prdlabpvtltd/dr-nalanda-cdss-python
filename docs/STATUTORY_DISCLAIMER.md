# Statutory Medical & Telemedicine Disclaimer

**Dr. Nalanda, MD** is a deterministic research and clinical decision support system developed by **Polygonal Research and Development Lab Private Limited** (Thrissur, Kerala, India).

### Regulatory Alignment:
- **National Medical Commission (NMC) Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020):**  
  Outputs generated by Dr. Nalanda are designed to support and assist registered medical practitioners. They do not substitute for clinical examination, formal medical judgment, or direct patient consultation.
- **US FDA 21 CFR Part 11 & Software as a Medical Device (SaMD):**  
  Complies with audit traceability, deterministic repeatability, and tamper-evident logging standards.
- **ISO 13485 & IEC 62304 Compliance:**  
  Engineered according to rigorous medical software life-cycle and risk management processes.
